Night Roll — Privacy

Draft, 2026-09-27. Applies to the Night Roll website and the Night Roll iPad app.

The short version

Night Roll has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. Your songs, annotations and settings stay on your device unless you choose to send them somewhere: your own GitHub repository, a folder you pick, or an AI service you configure.

What stays on your device

  • Songs you open, import or record; your annotations, drafts and settings. They live in the browser's storage on that device, and in the iPad app optionally in Files → On My iPad → Night Roll, if you turn that on.
  • Audio you record inside the app (audio tracks, dictated notes) is kept with the song on the device. Nothing is uploaded unless you publish the song.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask

  • GitHub. If you paste a GitHub token in Settings, the app talks to GitHub's API with it to read and publish songs and annotations in the repositories you name. The token is stored on your device and sent only to GitHub. Anything you publish to a public repository is public.
  • AI (✦ Ask). Off until you set it up. With the in-browser model, the model file is downloaded once from a public CDN and runs on your device; your questions do not leave it. With a remote backend, the app sends your question, the open song's notes and your annotations to the URL you entered — your own server or service, under its own terms. Night Roll ships no AI key and runs no AI service.
  • Dictation. The 🎤 button uses your device's built-in speech recognition. On Apple devices that is Apple's speech service, under Apple's terms; it may process audio on Apple's servers depending on your device settings.
  • Shared links. Opening a song link from someone else reads that song from their public GitHub repository.

Website hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages, and sounds and model files come from public CDNs. Those hosts see the ordinary requests any web page causes (your IP address, the files fetched). Night Roll adds no tracking to them.

Children

Night Roll collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
