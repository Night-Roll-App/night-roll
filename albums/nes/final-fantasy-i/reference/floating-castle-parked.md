## Status as of this writing

Tonic settled as Bb. Derivation is two-legged and non-circular:

1. **Corpus regularity.** The lowest-voice entry in bar 1 is the tonic in
   every song in the FF1 set — 18 for 18 once Prelude is counted correctly
   (Prelude has no triangle channel at all; pulse1 is the lowest voice and
   enters on A#3 against `key: Bb`). Zero exceptions. Floating Castle is the
   nineteenth instance and the only one where all three channels attack that
   pitch simultaneously — the full-chip unison already logged as a device in
   `analysis/floating-castle.md`.
2. **Melodic period.** Independent of the bass. See below.

**Open caveat on leg 1:** for the 18/18 to count as evidence rather than
bookkeeping, those keys must have been derived from chords independently
rather than set from the opening bass note. The well-worked songs (Town,
Cornelia Castle, Gurgu Volcano) appear chord-derived. This has not been
audited song by song. Until it is, leg 2 is what carries the derivation.

**Mode remains open and may not exist.** The whole-tone identification from
the prior session has not been reconciled with the Bb-tonic finding; the
analysis doc predates it.

## Observation 1 — the melodic period

pulse1 bars 5–8 are a note-for-note repeat of bars 1–4. Identical pitches,
identical rhythms. The single difference across the four bars is the chip
volume on the first note: v12 in bar 1, v15 in bar 5.

So the melody is a four-bar period stated twice, opening on A#5 both times.
Bar 5 is a downbeat return to the opening pitch at a completed phrase
boundary — a stronger claim than "the melody keeps coming back to Bb."

## Observation 2 — the non-whole-tone pitches

Bar 4 contains a B5 and an A5. Bar 9 contains a B5. These are the only
half-step neighbors to A#5 anywhere in pulse1, and they sit outside the
whole-tone collection the rest of the line occupies.

**The question to ask on return, not answered here:** where do these fall
relative to the A#5 arrivals? If they behave the way their placement
suggests, that is functional evidence for Bb rather than statistical
evidence, and it is stronger than any amount of repetition.

## Observation 3 — the second section behaves differently

Bars 9–12 pair off: 9 and 11 center A#5, 10 and 12 center D#5, as the same
shape transposed. Two centers a fourth apart.

Note the contrast with the bass in bars 1–8, which alternates two roots a
whole step apart, in exactly even two-beat halves. Different interval,
different rate.

**Unresolved:** whether the bars 9–12 alternation competes with Bb or
decorates it. This is a separate question from the tonic question that was
just settled, and settling the first does not settle this one.
