**AI (claude-code-sonnet):** Your instinct is close. In the key, the dominant seventh is built from scale degrees 5, 7, 2 and 4. The 5 is just the root, and the 2 is the chord's fifth, which is stable and often left out. The pull comes from the 7 and the 4, which sit a tritone apart. The 7 wants to step up to 1, and the 4 wants to step down to 3. That pair does most of the work, and it's why the seventh makes the dominant pull harder than a plain triad. A good way to test this by ear is to play the chord without the 4, then without the 7, and hear which one resolves more strongly.
