# Floating Castle

**Opened 2026-08-02 (chip capture). PARKED — tonic found, mode resists.**

## Confirmed

- **Tonic is Bb.** All three channels attack bar 1 beat 1 on Bb, spread
  across octaves. **Full-chip unison opening is unique in the corpus so
  far** — no other song opens this way. Logged as a device independent of
  the key question.

## The mode resists the question

Josh's ear says minor; the pitch content doesn't cooperate. His reading of
the bass figure: Bb, then E natural, then Eb. He proposed **Bb Lydian** — a
good hypothesis, E natural over Bb is exactly the Lydian #4 — then
correctly abandoned it when the Eb showed up in the same figure: raised 4th
and natural 4th within two beats.

Reframe to bring back to it: when a figure contains both versions of the
same degree in close proximity, "which mode is this" may be the wrong
question. Modes describe scales; some figures are built instead from an
**interval shape that gets transposed** — no mode fits because the composer
wasn't using one.

**The thing to try on return (deliberately not answered here):** the bass
figure has two halves — compare them *to each other* rather than fitting
either into a key.

## Context

Josh's read on intent: the Floating Castle is Sky Warrior technology, so
Uematsu may be writing *unfamiliarity* rather than menace — nothing for the
listener to orient by. If so, resisting a tonal reading is the point, and
"neither major nor minor, on purpose" is a legitimate verdict — the sweep
protocol's "unclear" category exists for exactly this.

(For the record: the Floating Castle is the second-to-last dungeon; Chaos
Shrine is the last and has its own theme.)
