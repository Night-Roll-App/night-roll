## Method note (general, learned here)

Reading only the chord's **onset beat** is a heuristic, not a rule — a
chord occupies its whole span and a tone can arrive late (5.3 broke the
heuristic: the D lands on beat 4). Read the full span; weight what's long
and metrically strong.
