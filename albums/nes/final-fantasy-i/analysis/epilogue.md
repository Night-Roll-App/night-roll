## Opening key: G major

Josh's verdict: the opening flourish uses G A B C D E F# with no F natural;
the bass settles onto a long sustained G pedal beneath it.

His structural observation, the important one: **this song is designed to
end, not loop.** Everything before the bass entry is a rising flourish that
never returns — textbook anacrusis. His framing: "if this song did loop, it
would loop back to that first bass note." That reading is what generalized
the sweep hypothesis (see key-sweep.md): the loop target was only ever a
machine-checkable proxy for the structural downbeat, and through-composed
pieces still have one — they just don't hand you the marker.

Sweep scoring: hit under the **general form**, flagged judgment-based
rather than data-verified. Prelude and Opening Theme will likely need the
same treatment.
