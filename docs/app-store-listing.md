**Description** [4000]:

Night Roll is a piano roll and score for studying music — starting with the music you already know by heart.

SEE EVERY VOICE
Open a song and every voice appears as bars on a piano roll, or as an engraved score, on one shared timeline. Tap a note and it plays. Loop a phrase. Slow it down without changing the pitch.

MARK WHAT YOU HEAR
Sections, chords, keys and loop points live as annotations anchored to beats, beside the song in a plain-text file you own. Nothing is pre-filled: the discoveries are yours. Ask "Chord?" only when you want a check.

WRITE YOUR OWN
New song, pencil in notes, pick a sound, add a recorded take as a track. Save into folders of your own on this iPad; publish to your own GitHub repository when you're ready, and open the same song on any device.

GAME MUSIC, THE REAL THING
Import a sound file from a game and hear it through the console's own sound hardware, emulated: NES, Game Boy and Super Nintendo play back with their real chips; Genesis, PlayStation and Nintendo 64 arrive as notes on the built-in voices. Every track lands as a song you can study, annotate and loop.

A TUTOR THAT HINTS
Ask questions about the open song — what's happening at the cursor, why a chord works, what to try next. The tutor sees the notes, your annotations and where you are. It hints before it tells, and it never writes an analysis you didn't ask for. It runs with a model of your choosing: in the app, on a server you run, or not at all.

STARTERS INCLUDED
Bach's Prelude in C, Satie's Gymnopédie No. 1, Für Elise and the first movement of the Moonlight Sonata, entered from the scores and annotated as demo material.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Your songs stay on your device unless you choose to publish them.
