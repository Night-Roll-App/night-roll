Icon audit

Night Roll — every on-screen glyph vs. its closest Google Material Icon

Josh asked for a pass over every icon in the app, now that Copy/Paste use real Google Material Icons (outlined, Apache-2.0) instead of the old ⧉/📋. This page lines up every button, menu item and chip glyph next to its closest Material match, says what the control does, and gives a one-line call: switch, keep, or either is fine.

Nothing in the app changes from this page — it's the menu to pick from, not a patch.
